This EAB paper addresses two CFP topics: "Benchmarking, performance modeling, tuning, and testing" and "Foundation models and LLMs for data engineering", with "Data quality, curation, provenance, and workflows".
(a) Challenge: systems store LLM outputs as tables, one row per segment of a transcript or document, and run aggregate queries on them. With identical words, moving segment boundaries changes answers: a chunker switch moves each article by 5.5-16 points for six open LLMs, with a sign that depends on the model.
(b) Contributions: segmentation-keyed views, invariance and drift (Sec. II); a preregistered benchmark with placebo control and non-LLM reference scorers, two domains, six scorers, all artifacts (Sec. III-VIII); view maintenance under re-segmentation (Sec. IX).
(c) It builds on view maintenance (SIGMOD'86), provenance (ICDT'01), Cyclex (ICDE'08), dataset versioning (PVLDB'16-17) and LLM-derived views (Evaporate, PVLDB'23; LOTUS, DocETL, PVLDB'25).
